Her voice is a setting, not a rebuild

You're hearing Ara, one of xAI's realtime voices. On a production build you pick any of them: the ElevenLabs Voice Library alone carries over 10,000 voices, on top of the stock voice sets from Hume, xAI, OpenAI and Deepgram. Swapping one is a single config value — her character doesn't change.

Want it to sound like a specific person? Voice cloning is supported for an additional fee, with their recorded consent.

This runs on a real-time speech-to-speech model, which is why she pauses, breathes and can be interrupted mid-sentence — go ahead and talk over her. The same engine puts an agent on real telephony with a dedicated number, call recording, transcripts and post-call analysis.
